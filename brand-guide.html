01 — Brand

What is Arko?

Positioning

The safety tool that works the way a site works.

Arko is built for safety inspectors in Georgia's construction industry — not for back-office teams. It's fast, offline-capable, and generates compliant documentation in seconds.

Fast
Offline-first
Georgian law
Safety-critical
Name Origin
ARKO — from arc: the structural form that bears load through geometry, not mass. A bridge. A vault. A form that holds. Arko carries this same precision — safety through structure.
Mission

Make construction sites safer through better documentation tools.

Audience

Safety inspectors, site supervisors, and construction companies in Georgia.

05 — Voice

How Arko speaks.

Direct
not corporate
No hedging. Every word earns its place. Short over long. Georgian inspectors are professionals — talk to them as such.
Confident
not arrogant
We know what we do. No hype, no overselling, zero exclamation marks. The product speaks for itself.
Precise
not technical
Exact language mirrors the product. Technical terms only when they're the right term — not to sound expert.
✓ Arko says
"Inspection complete. Report generated."
